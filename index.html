Thesis & Capstone Project

Glaucoma Diagnosis through AI-Generated Insights: A Dual Modality Perspective - OCT vs Fundus

Capstone Project · New Zealand

Glaucoma is a progressive optic neurone disease and it is among the major causes of blindness in the world. Prompt and proper diagnosis is the solution to loss of vision. Recent-past developments in artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning (DL) and generative models have enhanced greatly the accuracy and automation of glaucoma detection through the retinal imaging modalities. This review assesses the present trends and future possibilities of AI application in glaucoma diagnosis and particularly in multimodal imaging strategies based on fundus photography and Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT). We point out the shortcomings of existing DL models, present the generative AI techniques, and comment on the combination of fundus and OCT imaging to increase diagnostic accuracy. There are also some challenges over the ongoing issues of clinical translation, data constraints, interpretability, and the use of large multi-modal models described in the review. Lastly, we offer the future research potential that can result in more robust, explainable, and fair AI solutions in ophthalmology.

ADOPTION OF DATA ANALYTICS TOOLS FOR DECISION MAKING OF RETAIL BUSINESS IN NEPAL

Thesis · Nepal

This research study sheds light on the evolving landscape of decisionmaking in the retail sector, particularly within the context of the dynamic and competitive environment of e-commerce in Nepal. The insights gained from this study provide valuable information for retailers, policymakers and industry stakeholders looking to harness the potential of data analytics tools to drive effective decisionmaking processes in the retail business. The findings underscore the importance of embracing online analytical processing and advanced analytics while also suggesting that further exploration is needed to unlock the full potential of data warehousing and data marts in the retail sector.
